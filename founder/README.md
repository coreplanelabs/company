# About Boris Tane (Founder & CEO)

<img src="./boris.jpg" width="200">

Boris Tane is the founder of Coreplane Labs and the creator of Polylane, building tools that make AI-driven software deployment safer and more reliable. His work sits at the intersection of observability and AI agents, focused on preventing production incidents.

Before Coreplane Labs, Boris built [Baselime](https://baselime.io), an OpenTelemetry-native observability platform for serverless architectures, which [Cloudflare acquired in April 2024](https://blog.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-acquires-baselime-expands-observability-capabilities/). He then led the Workers Observability team at Cloudflare, building the Cloudflare Workers observability layer from scratch.

Boris writes about the future of software development, and is a long-time voice in the developer observability community. He is active on X ([@boristane](https://x.com/boristane)).
